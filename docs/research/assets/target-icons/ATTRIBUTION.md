# Target icon review assets

Current manifest: `../../target-icon-manifest.json`. These are local research / design-review assets and are not loaded by the application.

## GGG artwork (17 WebP files and one JPEG avatar)

Copyright belongs to **Grinding Gear Games**. The game images were obtained through [PoEDB](https://poedb.tw/us/); the GGG Atziri forum avatar was obtained from the [PoE Wiki archive](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/File:Atziri_forum_avatar.jpeg). Both are third-party sites; the art remains GGG property. They are **not** covered by the game-icons CC BY licence or by PoEDB text/wiki licences. See [GGG Terms of Use](https://www.pathofexile.com/legal/terms-of-use-and-privacy-policy) for the game IP and permitted uses; no general open-content redistribution licence is asserted here.

Downloaded bytes are unchanged: no recolouring, crop, text overlay or glyph substitution. The manifest records each actual source page, CDN asset URL, SHA-256 and source-page HTML SHA-256. The Essence image is the **homepage Essence league / minimap icon**, not a randomly selected Essence currency. After round-one review, support gems use the requested LesserMultipleProjectiles artwork, scarabs use an actual Ambush Scarab item image, and Vaal / Tormented Spirit use PoEDB category images. Category representatives do not predict specific drops or encounters.

## Game-icons SVG artwork

Icons made by **Lorc, Delapouite, Aussiesim and Skoll**. Available on https://game-icons.net. Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The upstream notice and full licence are preserved in `LICENSE.game-icons.txt` and `LICENSE-CC-BY-3.0.txt`.

Original source repository: https://github.com/game-icons/icons at commit `82d948812bfe3f269ef8f731dcdb07b08160edc4`. Per-file source links and modifications are in the manifest and SVG comments.

SVG adaptations remove the original black background and use currentColor. Some remaining SVGs contain composed glyphs. Six strongbox / sunken-treasure targets now share the same **unbadged** `strongbox.svg`. The treasure-anchor glyph remains unchanged.

Historical SVGs replaced by WebP or the shared chest remain in this folder for review history. Their embedded credits and the CC BY licence still apply; they are not referenced by the current manifest. Their old text marks do not appear in the current currency targets.

## Project-authored simple SVG marks

Eight review vector files were drawn in round one. Seven currently serve eleven target mappings: bathysphere, boss, altar-simple, coin-single, envelope-simple, magic-letter-m, and rare-letter-r; elite.svg is retained as history. Drawn in this project for the local review, with no third-party drawing paths or font dependency. These are not Game-icons artwork and are not described as PoE original art. Three explicit bosses share one boss image; three gold-related targets share one coin. Round two removes the coin inner ring. The sunken-totems target now uses the PoEDB Archnemesis minimap marker, solely as a user-selected visual symbol.
